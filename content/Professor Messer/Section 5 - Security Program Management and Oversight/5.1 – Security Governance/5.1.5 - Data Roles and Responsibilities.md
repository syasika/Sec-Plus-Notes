# Data Roles and Responsibilities

## Overview
- **Organizational Relationships**: Many individuals within an organization play different roles related to data management. These roles are not just technical but also involve aspects of data governance and accountability.

## 📌 High-Level Data Relationships
- *Many individuals* in an organization have different responsibilities for various aspects of data.
- These responsibilities fall under **organizational** rather than purely technical roles.

### 👥 Data Owner
- **Definition**: The individual ultimately accountable for specific data.
- **Typical Role**: Often a senior officer or executive within the organization.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Overseeing all aspects related to their area of data.
  - Bearing ultimate responsibility for the data.
- **Examples**:
  - *VP of Sales*: Owns customer relationship data.
  - *Treasurer*: Manages financial information.

### 👥 Data Controller
- **Definition**: Manages the purposes and means by which personal data is processed.
  - Decides why the data is being processed and how it will be handled.
- **Relationship to Data Processor**: Provides instructions to the processor on how the data should be used.
- **Example (Payroll)**:
  - *Payroll department*: Acts as the controller, defining payroll amounts, timeframes, and overall policy for employee payments.

### 👥 Data Processor
- **Definition**: Processes data on behalf of the data controller.
  - Executes instructions provided by the data controller.
- **Typical Role**: Often a third-party company or another internal group.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Accessing and using data as instructed by the controller.
  - Storing and handling employee information according to the controller's directives.
- **Example (Payroll)**:
  - *Payroll company (third-party)*: Acts as the processor, processing payroll, storing employee information, and handling bank details based on the payroll department's instructions.

### 👥 Data Custodian/Steward
- **Definition**: Responsible for data accuracy, privacy, and security.
- **Works Directly with the Data**: This role is more hands-on than the Data Owner.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Assigning sensitivity labels to data (e.g., confidential, public).
  - Ensuring compliance with applicable laws and standards (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
  - Managing access rights to determine which users have access to what types of data.
  - Implementing security controls to protect the data.
- **Examples**:
  - *VP of IT*: Ensures accurate and secure handling of sensitive information.

# 📝 Exam Takeaways

1. **Understand the Hierarchy and Relationships:**
   - **Data Owner**: Highest level of accountability (strategic, ultimate responsibility).
   - **Data Controller**: Decides the "what" and "how" of data processing.
   - **Data Processor**: Executes the processing as directed by the controller.
   - **Data Custodian/Steward**: Ensures data quality, security, and access control.

2. **Key Distinctions between Controller and Processor:**
   - **Controller**: Dictates the processing (purposes and means).
   - **Processor**: Performs the processing on behalf of the controller.
   - Think of it like an architect (controller) designing a house, and a builder (processor) constructing it according to the plans.

3. **Data Owner's Focus:**
   - Primarily accountability and broad oversight.
   - Often a senior business leader, not a technical role.
   - Example: VP of Sales for customer data, Treasurer for financial data.

4. **Data Custodian/Steward's Focus:**
   - Direct interaction with data to ensure its integrity and protection.
   - Key responsibilities include labeling data sensitivity, ensuring compliance, managing access rights, and implementing security controls.
   - This role bridges the gap between high-level policies (from the owner/controller) and technical implementation.

5. **Organizational vs. Technical:** Remember that these roles are primarily organizational responsibilities. While the Custodian/Steward has technical implementation duties, the Owner and Controller are more about strategic oversight and decision-making regarding data.

6. **Scenario-Based Questions:**
   - *Who decides if customer data can be shared with a marketing partner?* (Data Controller)
   - *Who is ultimately responsible if financial data is compromised?* (Data Owner - Treasurer)
   - *Who implements encryption on a database containing sensitive health records?* (Data Custodian/Steward)
   - *Who performs the actual data entry for a third-party service?* (Data Processor)

By focusing on these core definitions, responsibilities, and the relationships between roles, you should be well-prepared for any exam questions on this topic!
